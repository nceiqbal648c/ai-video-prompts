# Tropical Beach Sunset - AI Video Generation Prompt

## Description
A cinematic travel video of a beautiful tropical beach during sunset with golden light reflecting on ocean waves, gently swaying palm trees, and smooth drone motion.

## Prompt
A cinematic travel video of a beautiful tropical beach during sunset. Golden light reflecting on the ocean waves, palm trees gently swaying in the wind. A slow drone shot moving forward over the shoreline. Ultra-realistic, 4K quality, soft warm lighting, shallow depth of field, smooth motion, highly detailed, cinematic color grading.
